Algebra 1

Weekly practice for Algebra 1: 30 modules that follow along with your math class. Open the module your teacher assigned in Blackbaud, work through every step, then complete the Blackbaud check.

How it works

  • Every module has the same 7 steps: Context, Learn, Practice, Build, Reflect, Check, Challenge.
  • Learn has worked examples. Try each one on paper, then tap to see the steps.
  • Activities check themselves. Pink means try again. Yellow means your answer is equal but not finished (like a fraction that isn't reduced). Stuck? Tap Show a hint.
  • Type answers like 3x + 5, -2, 3/4 or x^2. The math keys under each box have ² √ ≤ ≥ and more.
  • Finish every activity to get the completion code for the Blackbaud check. The ★ Challenge at the end is optional, for when you're ready for more.
